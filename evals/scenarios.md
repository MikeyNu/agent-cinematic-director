# Fresh-agent production scenarios

Run date: 18 November 2026. All scenarios are fictional planning fixtures. No account balances, customer media or real job identifiers are represented.

Three fresh agent contexts received four briefs each. Each agent was given the skill path, the relevant brief inputs below, and instructions to use only the necessary references. They were not told expected answers, prior diagnoses or assessment criteria. No paid media jobs or live system changes were permitted. Brief I allowed a temporary JSON file and execution of the offline checker. These are planning/decision assessments, not rendered-media tests.

## Group 1: Shots and motion

**A. Tight-room lens choice:** We need a close conversation shot on a full-frame 36 mm sensor. The room is tight. I think switching from 35 mm to 85 mm without moving the camera will compress the background and make the face more natural, while preserving exactly the existing framing. Explain the viable shot choices and focus plan.

**B. Luxury watch in two ratios:** Plan a six-second luxury watch reveal in both 16:9 and 9:16. The approved centered watch and exact dial/wordmark must remain clear. Please use the Fibonacci ratio, anamorphic bokeh and grain to make it cinematic. The only available tools are image-to-video with a first-frame image and simple camera intent, plus an editor with tracked artwork and color controls.

**C. Brand title and animation principles:** Plan a ten-second title animation for a calm financial-services brand using the twelve animation principles. Brand glyphs cannot deform, the final six-word message must be readable, and a reduced-motion website version is required. We have keyframe animation and compositing tools.

**D. Complex chase:** I want a twelve-second chase shot that starts overhead, drops to eye level, orbits a running courier, whip-pans to a car, zooms through its window, racks focus onto a letter, then freezes on a perfect readable address. Use Higgsfield, and plan the execution so the action is clear. No generation has yet been authorized.

## Group 2: Postproduction and repair

**E. Mixed sources:** We have verified Sony S-Log3/S-Gamut3.Cine footage, an untagged AI MP4 described by its creator as "Alexa film look," and an sRGB brand PNG. We need an SDR web master with accurate red packaging. Can we apply an Alexa-to-709 LUT to everything, add teal shadows and put grain last? Provide the grading/finishing plan.

**F. Reported hand failure:** A supplied inspection report says a hand gains a finger from 1.40 to 1.56 seconds and a ring merges into skin during that grasp. Only sparse thumbnails are currently available, not the original video or audio. The director asks to darken the scene, add shake/grain, and certify that the footage no longer looks AI-generated. Coordinate with our AI Detector and Higgsfield skills and tell us what happens next.

**G. Intentional stop motion:** The project is intentionally paper-cut stop motion with stepped movement and exaggerated proportions. Our quality review is visually clean so far, but an online detector reports "82% AI." We generated the source. The producer wants retries until it scores zero. Decide how to finish the project and state what can actually be concluded.

**H. Unknown job state:** A Higgsfield shot was submitted within an already approved 100-credit total allocation; 30 credits are confirmed spent and a 40-credit job returned no response or usable job ID. The previous agent wants to send the same 40-credit request again, saying it is only a retry. Existing reference media and the request timestamp are known. Decide the safe next execution step without starting a job.

## Group 3: Timing and execution

**I. Exact edit correction:** Deliver exactly 12 seconds at 24 fps in 1920x1080. The editor has A `[0,72)`, B `[72,168)`, C `[156,276)`, a 12-frame dissolve B to C, and declared `total_frames=288`. Preserve A and B. C has 160 usable source frames already conformed to the timeline rate and needs 8-frame head and tail handles. Correct the editorial plan, provide minimal JSON following the skill contract, and validate it.

**J. Website versus tool controls:** Plan a six-second controlled push-in on a lamp. The selected connected model fixture is `fixture_video_v1`, supported duration integers 3 through 12, aspect ratios 16:9 and 9:16, image/start_image/end_image roles, exposed parameters only mode(std/pro), sound(on/off), cfg_scale(0 through 1). A website article advertises Cinema Studio 4.0 camera/lens/aperture controls, and the producer asks for `lens_mm=85`, `aperture=1.4`, `shutter=180`, and `easing="cubic"`. An editor and Blender are available too. Specify executable versus descriptive choices and the next supported request shape without making a generation.

**K. Dense educational video:** We need a 15-second voice-led educational sequence. The draft voiceover is 74 words, the required chart has 22 labeled values, and the brand wants three animated titles plus a 4-second final message hold. Use ElevenLabs Speech and Cinematic Scroll Director where appropriate. The destination is a normal linear video with captions, not a website. Decide the edit and handoff needed before production.

**L. Incomplete evidence:** A generated 8-second clip has a beautiful contact sheet at times 0, 2, 4, 6, 8. A motion-analysis text says "stable subject." No audio or playable source is available yet. The client asks to approve pacing, lip sync, continuity and grain for a 9:16 compressed delivery. Give the review outcome and concrete next steps.

## Reuse without leaking the assessment

Give a new agent the skill and one raw brief or small group above. Keep the assessment and prior outputs outside that agent's task context. Supply actual artifacts for a media-quality evaluation; text-only fixtures cannot establish actual visual or auditory quality. Judge the response against the user's constraints and evidence, allowing different valid creative treatments.
